# Original combat SFX

12 short procedural sounds generated by `scripts/generate-combat-sfx.py`.
No samples, music excerpts, external synthesizer presets, or third-party recordings.
Oscillators, deterministic noise and envelopes, encoded as mono MP3 for browser compatibility.
May be used/modified/distributed with this game; dedicated to CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Playback has a six-voice ceiling and 65ms per-key retrigger protection. Subject to existing SE mute/volume.
Listening on actual speakers/headphones and iOS Safari is still required; automated decoding does not establish audio quality.
